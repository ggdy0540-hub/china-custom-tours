Why visit Shanghai?

Shanghai is China's most cosmopolitan city — a place where 1920s art-deco mansions share a skyline with the world's second-tallest building, where French bakeries sit beside hole-in-the-wall noodle shops, and where the pace of reinvention never slows. It's the perfect beginning or ending to any China journey.

Must-See Sights

Iconic experiences in Shanghai

The Bund

Shanghai's iconic waterfront. Walk at blue hour when the Pudong skyline lights up across the Huangpu River.

Yu Garden & Old Town

A 400-year-old classical Chinese garden surrounded by teahouses, silk shops, and xiaolongbao restaurants.

French Concession

Tree-lined streets, hidden bars, indie boutiques, and the city's best brunch spots.

Shanghai Tower

The world's second-tallest building. Ride to the 118th floor for a 546-meter-high panoramic view.

What You'll Do

Recommended experiences

  • Bund waterfront walk at blue hour
  • Xiaolongbao soup dumpling trail
  • French Concession cycling and hidden-bar crawl
  • Shanghai Museum jade and calligraphy galleries
  • Maglev train ride at 430 km/h
  • Evening river cruise past the illuminated skyline
Best Season
March–May and October–November. Spring brings cherry blossoms; autumn is crisp and clear.
Local Flavors
Xiaolongbao soup dumplings, shengjian bao pan-fried buns, braised pork belly, hairy crab (autumn only), and Shanghainese dim sum.
Where to Stay
Heritage Bund hotels with river views, design boutiques in the French Concession, or Pudong towers above the clouds.
