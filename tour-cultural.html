Cultural Discovery

Walk in the footsteps of emperors, scribes, and revolutionaries. This journey connects China's three greatest cities through 5,000 years of civilization — from the Great Wall at dawn to the neon glow of Shanghai.

10 days Private, your group only 3 cities
Best for: First-time visitors, history lovers, and anyone who wants to understand China, not just see it.

Day by Day

Your journey, unfolding

Day 1–2

Beijing — Imperial Heart

  • Arrival and private transfer to your courtyard hotel
  • Forbidden City private tour with a history-scholar guide
  • Sunrise walk on the Great Wall at Mutianyu before the crowds
  • Hutong rickshaw tour and home-hosted dumpling dinner

Day 3–4

Beijing → Xi'an — Along the Ancient Road

  • Temple of Heaven at dawn with tai chi practitioners
  • High-speed train to Xi'an (4.5 hours)
  • Evening walk through the Muslim Quarter
  • Biangbiang noodles and yangrou paomo feast

Day 5–6

Xi'an — Warriors and Walls

  • Terracotta Army at opening time, up close
  • Cycle the 14 km ancient city wall at golden hour
  • Big Wild Goose Pagoda and Tang Dynasty show
  • Calligraphy class in a courtyard studio

Day 7–8

Xi'an → Shanghai — Past Meets Future

  • Flight to Shanghai (2.5 hours)
  • The Bund at blue hour
  • French Concession cycling and hidden-bar crawl
  • Shanghai Museum jade and calligraphy galleries

Day 9–10

Shanghai — The Grand Finale

  • Xiaolongbao breakfast trail
  • Yu Garden and Old Town exploration
  • Maglev train ride at 430 km/h
  • Farewell dinner with a river view
