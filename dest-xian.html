Why visit Xi'an?

Xi'an was China's capital for 13 dynasties and the eastern terminus of the Silk Road. Today it is a living museum where terracotta warriors stand guard, city walls encircle a vibrant Muslim Quarter, and the scent of cumin and lamb drifts from charcoal grills at dusk.

Must-See Sights

Iconic experiences in Xi'an

Terracotta Army

Over 8,000 life-sized clay soldiers, each face unique, guarding Emperor Qin Shi Huang's tomb for 2,200 years. We arrive at opening time for an uncrowded experience.

Ancient City Wall

One of the best-preserved city walls in China. Cycle the 14 km circuit at golden hour for panoramic views.

Muslim Quarter

A maze of lantern-lit streets where Hui Muslim culture thrives. Graze on roujiamo, biangbiang noodles, and pomegranate juice.

Big Wild Goose Pagoda

A 7th-century Buddhist pagoda and its surrounding gardens — peaceful at dusk.

What You'll Do

Recommended experiences

  • Terracotta Army at opening time, up close
  • Cycle the ancient city walls at sunset
  • Muslim Quarter street-food crawl
  • Tang Dynasty show and dumpling banquet
  • Calligraphy workshop visit
  • Day trip to Huashan mountain for hikers
Best Season
March–May and September–November. Spring brings plum blossoms; autumn paints the city walls gold.
Local Flavors
Roujiamo pork burger, biangbiang noodles, yangrou paomo lamb soup, pomegranate, and persimmon desserts.
Where to Stay
Boutique hotels inside the city wall put you steps from the Muslim Quarter.
