Why visit Chengdu?

Chengdu is the capital of easy living. Giant pandas snooze in bamboo groves, teahouses spill onto sidewalks, and the air hums with the clatter of mahjong tiles and the sizzle of Sichuan peppercorns. This is the city where China goes to relax — and to eat.

Must-See Sights

Iconic experiences in Chengdu

Giant Panda Base

See giant pandas and red pandas in a lush, natural setting. Arrive at 8 AM for feeding time — the cubs are at their most playful.

Jinli Ancient Street

A lively pedestrian lane of Qing-dynasty architecture, handicraft stalls, and Sichuan snacks.

Wuhou Shrine

A serene complex dedicated to Zhuge Liang, China's most brilliant strategist.

People's Park Teahouse

Chengdu's most iconic teahouse — grab a bamboo chair, order gaiwan tea, and watch the world go by.

What You'll Do

Recommended experiences

  • 'Panda keeper for a day' junior program
  • Dawn wet-market visit with a Chengdu chef
  • Sichuan hotpot and mapo tofu cooking class
  • Bamboo-grove teahouse afternoon
  • Sichuan Opera face-changing show
  • Day trip to Leshan Giant Buddha
Best Season
March–June and September–November. Chengdu is famously overcast. The pandas are most active in cool weather.
Local Flavors
Mapo tofu, kung pao chicken, Sichuan hotpot, dan dan noodles, and the unforgettable ma-la numbing-spicy flavor.
Where to Stay
Boutique courtyard hotels near the Wide and Narrow Alleys, or modern towers overlooking the Jin River.
