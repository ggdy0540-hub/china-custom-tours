Day by Day

Your journey, unfolding

Day 1–2

Guilin — Karst Kingdom

  • Arrival and transfer to your riverside hotel
  • Reed Flute Cave — surreal limestone formations
  • Sunset from Elephant Trunk Hill
  • Guilin rice noodle dinner

Day 3–4

Li River → Yangshuo

  • Sunrise Li River cruise past karst peaks
  • Bamboo rafting on the jade Yulong River
  • Cycling through Yangshuo's karst countryside
  • Impression Sanjie Liu light show

Day 5–6

Longji Rice Terraces

  • Drive to Dragon's Backbone rice terraces
  • Hike between Zhuang and Yao minority villages
  • Hilltop homestay with panoramic terrace views
  • Sunrise over the cascading paddies

Day 7–9

Zhangjiajie — Avatar Mountains

  • High-speed train to Zhangjiajie
  • Avatar Hallelujah Mountain and Bailong Elevator
  • Tianzi Mountain sunrise over the sea of clouds
  • Glass bridge across the Grand Canyon
  • Golden Whip Stream forest hike

Day 10–12

Fenghuang & Departure

  • Drive to the 1,300-year-old riverside town
  • Explore stilted wooden houses and stone bridges
  • Lantern-lit evening walk along the Tuo River
  • Tujia minority cultural evening
  • Departure transfer
