Why visit Guilin?

Guilin and Yangshuo are the China of scroll paintings — karst peaks rising like jade fingers from emerald rivers, fishermen on bamboo rafts, and rice paddies cascading down hillsides. This is the landscape that has inspired Chinese poets and painters for a thousand years.

Must-See Sights

Iconic experiences in Guilin

Li River Cruise

The 83 km cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo is one of the world's great boat journeys. Misty peaks, water buffalo, and cormorant fishermen drift past.

Yangshuo Countryside

Cycle through karst valleys, bamboo groves, and tiny villages. Stop for lunch at a farmhouse and swim in the Yulong River.

Longji Rice Terraces

Dragon's Backbone terraces carved into the mountains over 650 years. Hike between Zhuang and Yao minority villages.

Reed Flute Cave

A vast limestone cave filled with stalactites and stalagmites, dramatically lit in changing colors.

What You'll Do

Recommended experiences

  • Sunrise Li River cruise past karst peaks
  • Bamboo rafting on the jade Yulong River
  • Cycling through Yangshuo's karst countryside
  • Longji rice-terrace hike with village homestay
  • Cormorant fisherman encounter at dusk
  • Impression Sanjie Liu light show
Best Season
April–October. Spring brings mist and wildflowers; autumn offers golden rice terraces.
Local Flavors
Beer fish (Yangshuo specialty), Guilin rice noodles, oil tea, stuffed river snails, and pomelo.
Where to Stay
Riverside eco-lodges in Yangshuo, hilltop guesthouses in Longji, or comfortable hotels in Guilin city.
