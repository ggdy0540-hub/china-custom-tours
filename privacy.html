Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website or book a trip, we may collect:

  • Personal details — name, email address, phone number, nationality, passport information (for visa support), and travel preferences you provide via our forms or communications.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device information, and browsing patterns collected via cookies and analytics tools.
  • Payment data — processed securely through our payment providers; we never store full credit card numbers on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To design and deliver your tailor-made travel itinerary
  • To process bookings, payments, and visa support documents
  • To communicate with you about your trip and respond to inquiries
  • To improve our website, services, and customer experience
  • To comply with legal obligations in China and your country of residence

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We share data only with: hotel and transport partners necessary to deliver your trip; licensed guides assigned to your journey; payment processors for secure transactions; and legal authorities when required by law.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS), access controls, and secure storage to protect your data. All online payments are processed through PCI-compliant gateways. While no system is completely immune, we take every reasonable measure to safeguard your information.

5. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies for site functionality and optional analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the site. You may disable non-essential cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; request correction or deletion of your data; withdraw consent for data processing; request data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at zjonny338@gmail.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined above or as required by law. Booking records are retained for 5 years for tax and legal compliance. You may request earlier deletion subject to legal requirements.

8. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries: zjonny338@gmail.com or write to Jing · Meet China, 上海市静安区北京西路大厦.
