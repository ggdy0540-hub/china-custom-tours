Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before traveling to China with us. Can't find your answer? Just ask.

Booking & Planning
How do I start planning a trip?

Fill out our contact form or the customization form on any tour page. A travel designer will reply within 24 hours with initial ideas and a no-obligation quote. You can also book a free 30-minute video call.

Is there a minimum group size?

No. We design journeys for solo travelers, couples, families, and small groups alike. Every trip is 100% private — you'll never be mixed with strangers.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend 2–3 months in advance for the best hotel availability and guide selection. However, we can arrange trips in as little as 2 weeks if needed. Peak seasons (April–May, September–October, Chinese holidays) book up faster.

Can I make changes after booking?

Absolutely. Free itinerary revisions before final payment, and flexible on-trip adjustments whenever plans evolve. Your travel designer stays reachable throughout.

Visas & Documentation
Do I need a visa to visit China?

Most nationalities require a tourist visa (L visa). We provide official invitation letters and all documentation you need for your application. Citizens of 50+ countries can now enjoy 15-day visa-free entry, and 72/144-hour transit visa exemptions are available for many routes.

How long does the visa process take?

Typically 4–5 business days at a Chinese embassy or visa center. Some countries offer mail-in applications. We'll walk you through the exact process for your nationality.

On Your Trip
Will I have a guide the entire time?

Yes. A licensed, English-speaking guide accompanies you in each city. For multi-city trips, you'll have a different local specialist in each destination — all coordinated by your dedicated travel designer.

Is it safe to travel in China?

China is one of the safest countries for international travelers. Violent crime is extremely rare. Petty theft is uncommon. Our 24/7 support line ensures help is always a call away.

What about internet access?

Most international apps (Google, WhatsApp, Instagram, etc.) are blocked in China. We provide a pre-trip connectivity guide and can arrange a portable Wi-Fi device and VPN for you. All hotels have Wi-Fi.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes — vegetarian, vegan, halal, kosher, gluten-free, and allergy-specific needs are all accommodated. Just tell us in advance; your guide will communicate with every restaurant.

Payments & Cancellation
How do I pay?

We accept bank transfer, credit card (Visa/Mastercard), and PayPal. A 30% deposit secures your booking; the balance is due 30 days before departure. Full details are in your proposal.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 45 days before departure. 30–44 days: 30% fee. 15–29 days: 50% fee. Under 15 days: 80% fee. We strongly recommend travel insurance, which we can help arrange.

Still have questions?

No question is too small. Reach out and we'll get back to you within 24 hours — usually much faster.
